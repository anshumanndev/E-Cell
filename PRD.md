# Product Requirements Document (PRD)

# IlluminatE × E-Cell UIT Website

## 1. Product Overview

**Product:** IlluminatE × E-Cell UIT Website

**Organization:** E-Cell UIT

**Institution:** United Institute of Technology, Prayagraj

**Core tagline:** UNPLUG. INNOVATE. BREAK.

**Secondary tagline:** Empowering the Next Generation of Changemakers.

**Product type:** Premium event + entrepreneurship ecosystem website.

## 2. Product Vision

Create a cinematic digital experience that introduces IlluminatE,
communicates the value of entrepreneurship and innovation, presents the
featured event, encourages registration, and connects visitors with the
wider E-Cell UIT ecosystem.

The website must feel like:

-   A premium startup website.
-   A national entrepreneurship event.
-   A futuristic editorial experience.
-   A college innovation ecosystem.

It must not feel like a generic college society website.

## 3. Problem Statement

A conventional event website usually provides information but does not
create excitement or communicate a strong identity.

IlluminatE needs to do both:

1.  Explain what the event is.
2.  Make visitors want to participate.
3.  Make registration obvious.
4.  Establish E-Cell UIT credibility.
5.  Show the broader entrepreneurial community.
6.  Provide a foundation for future events and content management.

## 4. Target Users

### Primary

-   Students interested in entrepreneurship.
-   Students interested in startups.
-   Students interested in innovation.
-   Students interested in technology/design/business.
-   Students looking for networking opportunities.
-   Students interested in leadership.

### Secondary

-   Campus ambassadors.
-   Potential E-Cell members.
-   Speakers.
-   Industry/founder participants.
-   Institutional stakeholders.
-   Partners and sponsors.

### Admin

-   E-Cell organizers.
-   Authorized event administrators.

## 5. Business/Product Goals

### Goal 1 --- Awareness

A first-time visitor should understand what IlluminatE is within
approximately 10 seconds.

### Goal 2 --- Conversion

Make registration the clearest next action.

### Goal 3 --- Trust

Present verified event, institution, speaker and community information.

### Goal 4 --- Community

Encourage visitors to join E-Cell UIT beyond the event.

### Goal 5 --- Scalability

Allow future events, speakers, team members and gallery items to be
managed without rewriting the public website.

## 32. Functional Requirements

### FR-01 Navigation

Users can navigate to all primary sections.

### FR-02 Registration

Users can complete a three-step registration flow.

### FR-03 Event Information

Event information is displayed from structured data.

### FR-04 Speaker Management

Verified speakers can be displayed without code changes after backend
implementation.

### FR-05 Gallery

Users can filter gallery categories.

### FR-06 FAQ

Users can expand/collapse questions.

### FR-07 Responsive UI

All public functionality works on desktop, tablet and mobile.

### FR-08 Admin

Authorized admins can manage events, speakers, registrations, team and
gallery.

### FR-09 Export

Admins can export registration records as CSV.

### FR-10 Success Pass

Successful registration displays a QR-based pass interface.

## 33. Non-Functional Requirements

-   Fast first load.
-   Optimized images.
-   Minimal unnecessary JavaScript.
-   Mobile-friendly animations.

## 40. Final Product Principle

The website should answer five questions within the first 10 seconds:

1.  **What is it?** --- IlluminatE by E-Cell UIT.
2.  **Why care?** --- Entrepreneurship, innovation, networking and
    opportunities.
3.  **What's happening?** --- Verified speaker/founder opportunity and
    event experience.
4.  **What do I get?** --- Learning, exposure, networking, certificate
    and event benefits as officially applicable.
5.  **What do I do?** --- Register.

The final experience should make the visitor feel:

**curious → inspired → informed → excited → ready to act.**
